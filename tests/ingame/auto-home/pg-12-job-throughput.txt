# id: PG-job-throughput-12
# covers: PG 177 (Labouring 50: no gear vs +50% Labouring gear on a worker-driven job; the Phase 8 "do jobs read the hooked stat" proof)
# fixture: auto-home
# reset: fresh
# needs: ProfessionGearProgression.dll 7D80DBB3 (JobOperateScaling on by default); harness with `produced`, `teleport <npc> building`, `building/job ... radius`; set_test_mode.ps1 -Mode Forced -Rules InGameTest (before the launch)
# history: m13 + m14 used the powered Stone Mine: 0.47-0.48 stone/h in every window, also with operate scaling on. A powered mine runs at the machine's own rate (output_rate=0.1), the worker does not drive it: not a skill benchmark. v3 uses the worker-driven Manual Stone Processor (Raw Stone -> Building Materials).
# history v3 (m15): counters were uninitialised (VS2010 struct init bug, fixed) and the processor ran out of Raw Stone in window A (Malzin's bag was full: give 0/10), so A/B/C were not comparable. Raw summed amount per game minute: A 6.6, B 7.3, C 7.4 (C scaled 11.1).
# history v4 (m16, 10C19BAB): A no gear output_progress 0.1065, B +50% scaling off 0.1123 (x1.05), C +50% scaling on 0.1695 (x1.59): the job reads the raw skill; JobOperateScaling is now on by default.
# verify: v5 with the default build: B (+50% gear) output_progress ~1.5 x A = row 177 PASS (the fix works by default); C (gear, scaling switched off) ~ A (control). calls similar in all windows.
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
health Malzin 100
health Shay 100
hunger Malzin 280
hunger Shay 280
teleport Shay building "Manual Stone Processor" dist 20
teleport Malzin building "Manual Stone Processor" dist 6
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ product=
clearjobs Malzin
pg_clear Malzin ~ cleared affixes
setstat Malzin labouring 50
pg_bonus Malzin labouring ~ equipped_bonus=0% base=50\.00 .*match=1
# input stone: the worker carries it in from her own bag
give Malzin "Raw Stone" 20
job Malzin "Manual Stone Processor" radius 1000 ~ Manual Stone Processor
pg_jobscale ~ jobOperateScaling=1
# warm-up: she walks over, loads stone and starts working
speed 10
@wait-game 15 900
speed 0
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ calls=[1-9]
# --- A: no Labouring gear (scaling on changes nothing without a bonus) ---
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" reset radius 100 near Malzin ~ \(reset\)
speed 10
@wait-game 30 900
speed 0
@set OA pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ scaled_amount=[\d.]+ avg_amount=[\d.]+ .*output_progress=[\d.]+)
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ full=0 .*state=NORMAL
hunger Malzin 280
# --- B: +50% Labouring gear, default build (JobOperateScaling on) ---
pg_force_affix Malzin "Black Cloth Shirt" labouring 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=Labouring:50
pg_bonus Malzin labouring ~ equipped_bonus=50% base=50\.\d\d .*match=1
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" reset radius 100 near Malzin ~ \(reset\)
speed 10
@wait-game 30 900
speed 0
@set OB pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ scaled_amount=[\d.]+ avg_amount=[\d.]+ .*output_progress=[\d.]+)
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ full=0 .*state=NORMAL
hunger Malzin 280
# --- C (control): +50% Labouring gear with JobOperateScaling switched off ---
pg_jobscale off ~ jobOperateScaling=0
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" reset radius 100 near Malzin ~ \(reset\)
speed 10
@wait-game 30 900
speed 0
@set OC pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ scaled_amount=[\d.]+ avg_amount=[\d.]+ .*output_progress=[\d.]+)
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ full=0 .*state=NORMAL
pg_jobscale on ~ jobOperateScaling=1
@echo A (no gear): ${OA}
@echo B (+50% Labouring, default build): ${OB}
@echo C (+50%, scaling switched off): ${OC}
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ product=
clearjobs Malzin
