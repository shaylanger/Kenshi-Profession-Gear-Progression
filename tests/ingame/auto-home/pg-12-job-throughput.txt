# id: PG-job-throughput-12
# covers: PG 177 (Labouring 50: no gear vs +50% Labouring gear on the real Stone Mine job; the Phase 8 "do jobs read the hooked stat" proof)
# fixture: auto-home
# reset: fresh
# needs: ProfessionGearProgression.dll 05E41322 (pg_operate/pg_jobscale + operate hooks); harness C5804B0C (`produced`, `teleport <npc> building`, `building/job ... radius`); set_test_mode.ps1 -Mode Forced -Rules InGameTest (before the launch)
# verify: three equal 2-game-hour windows on the Stone Mine: A no gear, B +50% Labouring (scaling off), C +50% with JobOperateScaling on. Compare per window: pg_operate output_gain (fractional output, not just whole stones), avg_amount and calls, plus produced per_game_hour. B > A = the job reads the hooked stat (row 177 PASS without a job hook). B ~ A and C ~ 1.5 x A = the job reads the raw skill; the operate-scaling hook is the fix (turn it on by default). C ~ A as well = output is limited by the machine, not the worker.
# history: m13 (A/B, whole stones only) gave 0.47 vs 0.48 stone per game hour: too coarse (1 stone per window)
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
health Malzin 100
health Shay 100
hunger Malzin 280
hunger Shay 280
teleport Shay building "Stone Mine" dist 20
teleport Malzin building "Stone Mine" dist 8
building "Stone Mine" 1000 ~ product=
clearjobs Malzin
pg_clear Malzin ~ cleared affixes
setstat Malzin labouring 50
pg_bonus Malzin labouring ~ equipped_bonus=0% base=50\.00 .*match=1
job Malzin "Stone Mine" radius 1000 ~ Stone Mine
# warm-up: she walks over and starts working
pg_jobscale off ~ jobOperateScaling=0
speed 10
@wait-game 15 900
speed 0
produced "Stone Mine" reset radius 1000
# --- A: no Labouring gear ---
pg_operate "Stone Mine" reset ~ Stone Mine
produced "Stone Mine" reset radius 1000
speed 10
@wait-game 120 1800
speed 0
@set OA pg_operate "Stone Mine" ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ .*output_gain=-?[\d.]+)
@set RA produced "Stone Mine" radius 1000 ~ per_game_hour=([\d.]+)
hunger Malzin 280
# --- B: +50% Labouring gear, no operate scaling ---
pg_force_affix Malzin "Black Cloth Shirt" labouring 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=Labouring:50
pg_bonus Malzin labouring ~ equipped_bonus=50% base=50\.\d\d .*match=1
pg_operate "Stone Mine" reset ~ Stone Mine
produced "Stone Mine" reset radius 1000
speed 10
@wait-game 120 1800
speed 0
@set OB pg_operate "Stone Mine" ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ .*output_gain=-?[\d.]+)
@set RB produced "Stone Mine" radius 1000 ~ per_game_hour=([\d.]+)
hunger Malzin 280
# --- C: +50% Labouring gear with JobOperateScaling on ---
pg_jobscale on ~ jobOperateScaling=1
pg_operate "Stone Mine" reset ~ Stone Mine
produced "Stone Mine" reset radius 1000
speed 10
@wait-game 120 1800
speed 0
@set OC pg_operate "Stone Mine" ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ .*output_gain=-?[\d.]+)
@set RC produced "Stone Mine" radius 1000 ~ per_game_hour=([\d.]+)
pg_jobscale off ~ jobOperateScaling=0
@echo A (no gear): ${OA} | stone/h ${RA}
@echo B (+50% Labouring): ${OB} | stone/h ${RB}
@echo C (+50%, operate scaling on): ${OC} | stone/h ${RC}
pg_operate "Stone Mine" ~ worker=Malzin
clearjobs Malzin
