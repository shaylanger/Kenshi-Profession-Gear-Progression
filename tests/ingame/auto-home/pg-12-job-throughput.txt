# id: PG-job-throughput-12
# covers: PG 177 (Labouring 50: no gear vs +50% Labouring gear on a worker-driven job; the Phase 8 "do jobs read the hooked stat" proof)
# fixture: auto-home
# reset: fresh
# needs: ProfessionGearProgression.dll E57B9E8E (pg_operate radius/near + operate hooks); harness with `produced`, `teleport <npc> building`, `building/job ... radius`; set_test_mode.ps1 -Mode Forced -Rules InGameTest (before the launch)
# history: m13 + m14 used the powered Stone Mine: 0.47-0.48 stone/h in every window, also with operate scaling on. A powered mine runs at the machine's own rate (output_rate=0.1), the worker does not drive it: not a skill benchmark. v3 uses the worker-driven Manual Stone Processor (Raw Stone -> Building Materials).
# verify: three equal 2-game-hour windows: A no gear, B +50% Labouring (scaling off), C +50% with JobOperateScaling on. Compare pg_operate per window: calls (> 0 proves the worker drove the machine), avg_amount, output_gain (fractional output). B > A = the job reads the hooked stat (row 177 PASS, no job hook needed). B ~ A and C ~ 1.5 x A = the job reads the raw skill: operate scaling is the fix. calls=0 or C ~ A = this machine is not worker-driven either: row 177 NEEDS-SETUP.
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
health Malzin 100
health Shay 100
hunger Malzin 280
hunger Shay 280
teleport Shay building "Manual Stone Processor" dist 20
teleport Malzin building "Manual Stone Processor" dist 6
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ product=
clearjobs Malzin
pg_clear Malzin ~ cleared affixes
setstat Malzin labouring 50
pg_bonus Malzin labouring ~ equipped_bonus=0% base=50\.00 .*match=1
# input stone: the worker carries it in from her own bag
give Malzin "Raw Stone" 20
job Malzin "Manual Stone Processor" radius 1000 ~ Manual Stone Processor
pg_jobscale off ~ jobOperateScaling=0
# warm-up: she walks over, loads stone and starts working
speed 10
@wait-game 15 900
speed 0
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ calls=[1-9]
# --- A: no Labouring gear ---
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" reset radius 100 near Malzin ~ \(reset\)
speed 10
@wait-game 120 1800
speed 0
@set OA pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ .*output_gain=-?[\d.]+)
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ full=0
hunger Malzin 280
give Malzin "Raw Stone" 10
# --- B: +50% Labouring gear, no operate scaling ---
pg_force_affix Malzin "Black Cloth Shirt" labouring 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=Labouring:50
pg_bonus Malzin labouring ~ equipped_bonus=50% base=50\.\d\d .*match=1
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" reset radius 100 near Malzin ~ \(reset\)
speed 10
@wait-game 120 1800
speed 0
@set OB pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ .*output_gain=-?[\d.]+)
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ full=0
hunger Malzin 280
give Malzin "Raw Stone" 10
# --- C: +50% Labouring gear with JobOperateScaling on ---
pg_jobscale on ~ jobOperateScaling=1
pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" reset radius 100 near Malzin ~ \(reset\)
speed 10
@wait-game 120 1800
speed 0
@set OC pg_operate "Manual Stone Processor" radius 100 near Malzin ~ (calls=\d+ amount=[\d.]+ .*output_gain=-?[\d.]+)
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ full=0
pg_jobscale off ~ jobOperateScaling=0
@echo A (no gear): ${OA}
@echo B (+50% Labouring): ${OB}
@echo C (+50%, operate scaling on): ${OC}
building "Manual Stone Processor" 1000 ~ product=
clearjobs Malzin
