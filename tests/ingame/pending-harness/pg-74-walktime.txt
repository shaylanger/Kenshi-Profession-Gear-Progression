# id: PG-walktime-74
# covers: PG 254 (Athletics footwear: real movement speed uses the effective stat; the base skill is not changed)
# fixture: auto-home
# reset: fresh
# needs: harness KAH 19 (`walktime <npc> <dist>`: the npc runs <dist> m in a straight line, the reply gives the game
#   seconds taken; assumed `seconds=<s>`); PG 7D80DBB3+; Forced + InGameTest
# verify: time with +50% Athletics < time without (3 runs each, echoed); `stat Malzin athletics` base unchanged at the end
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
health Malzin 100
hunger Malzin 280
pg_clear Malzin ~ cleared affixes
setstat Malzin athletics 30
@set T1 walktime Malzin 60 ~ (seconds=[\d.]+)
@set T2 walktime Malzin 60 ~ (seconds=[\d.]+)
@set T3 walktime Malzin 60 ~ (seconds=[\d.]+)
pg_force_affix Malzin "Black Cloth Shirt" athletics 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=Athletics:50
pg_bonus Malzin athletics ~ equipped_bonus=50% .*match=1
@set G1 walktime Malzin 60 ~ (seconds=[\d.]+)
@set G2 walktime Malzin 60 ~ (seconds=[\d.]+)
@set G3 walktime Malzin 60 ~ (seconds=[\d.]+)
stat Malzin athletics ~ base=3[01].
@echo no gear: ${T1} ${T2} ${T3}
@echo +50% Athletics: ${G1} ${G2} ${G3}
