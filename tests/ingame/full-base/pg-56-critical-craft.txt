# fixture: Testing-Save-Full-Base (squad Beaks + Avarek; weapon smith)

# needs: harness 616121b+ (benches near <x y z>, fill topup); ProfessionGearProgression.dll D7A60E49 or later (`pg_force_critical`, hook on CraftingBuilding::calculateCriticalChance),

# design: two REAL Sickle crafts by Avarek at weapon smithing 10 (low quality): first normal, then with pg_force_critical on.
#   A critical success makes the game pick a better weapon grade (getCriticalSuccessWeapon), so the second roll line must
#   show the better finished quality and a tier at least as high.
# verify (ProfessionGear.log of this run): `force critical: chance <vanilla> -> 1 smith=Avarek` appears only after `on`;
#   two `roll source=crafted ... name="...Sickle..."` lines; the second (critical) has tier >= the first, and its tier is the
#   one pg_check's rule gives for the better grade (row 89 PASS). `messages` may also show the game's critical note.
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
wake Avarek
speed 1 hold
@sleep 4
speed 0
protect Beaks on
protect Avarek on
hunger Avarek 280
select Avarek
clearjobs Avarek
pg_force_critical off ~ forceCritical=0$
research "Basic Weapon Smithing"
research "Basic Weapon Grades"
research "Utility Weapons"
research "Katanas"
research "Basic Weapon Grades 3"
speed 1 hold
@sleep 2
speed 0
wake Avarek
speed 1 hold
@sleep 3
speed 0
@until 30 teleport Avarek building "Weapon Smith" dist 4 radius 1500 ~ moved=1
@until 30 teleport Beaks Avarek dist 8 ~ moved=1
benches 60 crafts ~ Weapon[^|]*Sickle
# m24-4080: benches/fill search around the first squad member (Full-Base: Avarek); he left the bench at speed 50,
#   so the queue is polled at the bench position
@set WX buildings 60 smith ~ Weapon Smith V dist=\S+ pos=([-\d.]+),[-\d.]+,[-\d.]+
@set WY buildings 60 smith ~ Weapon Smith V dist=\S+ pos=[-\d.]+,([-\d.]+),[-\d.]+
@set WZ buildings 60 smith ~ Weapon Smith V dist=\S+ pos=[-\d.]+,[-\d.]+,([-\d.]+)
setstat Avarek weapon_smith 10
# --- normal craft ---
# m24-4080: bench inputs take nothing until a craft is queued (m23 probe) -> craft first, then fill
craft Avarek "Sickle" at "Weapon Smith" ~ queued 1x
fill "Weapon Smith" "Steel Bars" 10 radius 60
fill "Weapon Smith" "Fabrics" 10 radius 60
speed 50 hold
@until 900 benches 30 near ${WX} ${WY} ${WZ} ~ Weapon[^|]*queue=0

where Avarek
where Beaks
