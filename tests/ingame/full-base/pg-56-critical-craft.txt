# covers: PG 89 (critical-success craft: the actual finished quality drives the tier)
# fixture: Testing-Save-Full-Base (squad Beaks + Avarek; weapon smith)
# reset: fresh
# needs: ProfessionGearProgression.dll D7A60E49 or later (`pg_force_critical`, hook on CraftingBuilding::calculateCriticalChance),
#   harness 08AB6BF0; set_test_mode.ps1 -Mode Forced -Rules InGameTest
# design: two REAL Sickle crafts by Avarek at weapon smithing 10 (low quality): first normal, then with pg_force_critical on.
#   A critical success makes the game pick a better weapon grade (getCriticalSuccessWeapon), so the second roll line must
#   show the better finished quality and a tier at least as high.
# verify (ProfessionGear.log of this run): `force critical: chance <vanilla> -> 1 smith=Avarek` appears only after `on`;
#   two `roll source=crafted ... name="...Sickle..."` lines; the second (critical) has tier >= the first, and its tier is the
#   one pg_check's rule gives for the better grade (row 89 PASS). `messages` may also show the game's critical note.
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
protect Beaks on
protect Avarek on
hunger Avarek 280
select Beaks
clearjobs Avarek
pg_force_critical off ~ forceCritical=0$
research "Basic Weapon Smithing"
research "Basic Weapon Grades"
research "Utility Weapons"
benches 500 crafts ~ Weapon[^|]*Sickle
setstat Avarek weapon_smith 10
# --- normal craft ---
give Avarek "Iron Plates" 10
craft Avarek "Sickle" at "Weapon" ~ queued 1x
speed 10
@until 400 benches 500 ~ Weapon[^|]*queue=0
speed 0
@log D:\Steam\steamapps\common\Kenshi\mods\ProfessionGearProgression\ProfessionGear.log ~ roll source=crafted key=pgp\S+ baseId=\S+ name="[^"]*Sickle[^"]*" equipped=0 tier=\d
# --- forced critical craft ---
pg_force_critical on ~ forceCritical=1$
give Avarek "Iron Plates" 10
craft Avarek "Sickle" at "Weapon" ~ queued 1x
speed 10
@until 400 benches 500 ~ Weapon[^|]*queue=0
speed 0
pg_force_critical off ~ forceCritical=0$
@log D:\Steam\steamapps\common\Kenshi\mods\ProfessionGearProgression\ProfessionGear.log ~ force critical: chance [\d.e-]+ -> 1 smith=Avarek
messages 10
clearjobs Avarek
