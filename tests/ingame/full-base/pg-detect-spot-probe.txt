# pg-detect-spot-probe (m31-5090): find a fixed open spot for the stealth/perception gates (87/89).
# For each candidate (Beaks teleported N along x from the pinned Avarek), a Hungry Bandit is pinned 100 units
# along x from Beaks facing him and `face` reports los. Pick a spot with los >= 0.5 (ideally ~1.0) at both
# 100 and 110 units, then set DETECT_SPOT = (x, y, z) from its `where Beaks` line in tools/balance_driver.py
# and regenerate (python3 tools/balance_driver.py gen; ... gate). Needs harness A447F08E+ (face los=).
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
wake Beaks
speed 1 hold
@sleep 4
speed 0
protect Avarek on
protect Beaks on
select Beaks
clearjobs Beaks
wake Avarek
pin Avarek ~ pinned
# --- candidate 1: Avarek dist 60 ---
@until 30 teleport Beaks Avarek dist 60 ~ moved=1
speed 1 hold
@sleep 2
speed 0
@set W where Beaks ~ (.+)
@set OBS spawn "Hungry Bandit" Drifters near Beaks dist 100 count 1 ~ spawned 1/1 [^:]+: .+? (#\d+(?:/\d+)?)
setname ${OBS} PGP1
relation PGP1 0
pin PGP1 at Beaks dist 100 face Beaks ~ pinned
speed 1 hold
@sleep 2
speed 0
@set L face PGP1 Beaks ~ (dist=\S+ observer_ko=\d los=\S+)
@echo PROBE,1,60,${L},${W}
pin PGP1 off
kill PGP1
teleport PGP1 Beaks dist 300
