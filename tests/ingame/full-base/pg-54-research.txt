# id: PG-research-54
# covers: PG 184 (Science: research speed vs raw skill and vs +50% Science gear, with a Labouring control)
# fixture: Testing-Save-Full-Base (squad Beaks + Avarek; research bench)
# reset: fresh
# needs: harness 6BA59614+ (KAH 14 `research start|status`), PG 7D80DBB3+; Forced + InGameTest
# design: Avarek researches at the bench; 5 windows of 10 game min at 10x: Science 10 / 50 / 90 without gear, Science 50
#   with +50% Science, Science 50 with +50% Labouring (control). progress (0..1) of the queued tech is read before/after.
#   The tech: "Research Bench II" is a guess; if `research start` refuses it, pick another from `find research` that
#   is not done yet (the reply says why) and tell the PG agent.
# verify: progress rises in every window (else no power/bench level: see `research status` desk_level/benches);
#   gain grows with Science; +50% Science vs the Science 50 window shows whether research reads the hooked stat
#   (ResearchBuilding::operate is not scaled by PG); Labouring control = no change.
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
protect Beaks on
protect Avarek on
hunger Avarek 280
select Beaks
clearjobs Avarek
research status
research start "Research Bench II" ~ queue=[1-9]
give Avarek "Iron Hat" 1
equip Avarek "Iron Hat"
job Avarek "Research" radius 500
speed 10
@wait-game 5 600
speed 0
# --- s10 ---
setstat Avarek science 10
pg_clear Avarek ~ cleared affixes
@set P0 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
speed 10
@wait-game 10 900
speed 0
@set P1 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
@echo RES,184,science,10,0,600,${P0},${P1},none,s10
hunger Avarek 280
# --- s50 ---
setstat Avarek science 50
pg_clear Avarek ~ cleared affixes
@set P0 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
speed 10
@wait-game 10 900
speed 0
@set P1 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
@echo RES,184,science,50,0,600,${P0},${P1},none,s50
hunger Avarek 280
# --- s90 ---
setstat Avarek science 90
pg_clear Avarek ~ cleared affixes
@set P0 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
speed 10
@wait-game 10 900
speed 0
@set P1 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
@echo RES,184,science,90,0,600,${P0},${P1},none,s90
hunger Avarek 280
# --- s50_sci50 ---
setstat Avarek science 50
pg_clear Avarek ~ cleared affixes
pg_force_affix Avarek "Iron Hat" science 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=
pg_bonus Avarek science ~ equipped_bonus=50% .*match=1
@set P0 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
speed 10
@wait-game 10 900
speed 0
@set P1 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
@echo RES,184,science,50,50,600,${P0},${P1},science,s50_sci50
hunger Avarek 280
# --- s50_lab50 ---
setstat Avarek science 50
pg_clear Avarek ~ cleared affixes
pg_force_affix Avarek "Iron Hat" labouring 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=
pg_bonus Avarek labouring ~ equipped_bonus=50% .*match=1
@set P0 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
speed 10
@wait-game 10 900
speed 0
@set P1 research status ~ progress=([\d.]+)
@echo RES,184,science,50,50,600,${P0},${P1},labouring,s50_lab50
hunger Avarek 280
research status
clearjobs Avarek
