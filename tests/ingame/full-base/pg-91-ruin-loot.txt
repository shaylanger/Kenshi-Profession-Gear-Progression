#   afterwards -Sidecar Restore). Forced mode would make every eligible item roll: the result means nothing there.
# design: part B runs first (at the base), then part A. Part A, real ruins: the 3 ruins/labs nearest the base (harness `towns ruin,ancient,...`) are visited by teleport
#   (both squad members, protected); after 20 s at speed 1 (streaming) every item in the non-player buildings within
#   250 m is given its world-loot roll as if Avarek picked it up (`pg_lootscan`: the same EnsureRecord call the
#   character scan makes, Avarek as the finder). A second scan of the same place must find every eligible item
#   already recorded (no re-roll). Part B, a harness-made chest corpus (always available): 300 m from the base a
#   5 General Camp Storage Chests owned by Drifters are built and filled with 15 common gear types x 3, then scanned.
# verify (250): per scan line items / eligible / rolled / stats. Pass when, over all scans:
#   - eligible > 0 and the rolled share of eligible is between 0.15 and 0.6 (world loot rolls at half the tier
#     chance: .10-.48 by tier; a share above 0.6 = excessive frequency, below 0.15 = loot is pointless);
#   - the affixes cover at least 4 different stats (a useful mix), and no single stat has more than 50% of them;
#   - in ProfessionGear.log every new line for these items says `roll source=world_loot` (never npc/trader), and
#     each rolled item's stats belong to its tags (coherent gear: Rattan/Straw Hat farming, Ninja Rags stealth, ...);
#   - the second scan of each ruin: already_recorded = eligible, rolled unchanged.
#   Summary: python3 tools/analyze_loot.py <ProfessionGear.log> (world_loot lines: shares by tier, stat mix, tags).
# A ruin that `towns` lists but has no containers in range gives items=0: fine, the chest corpus still counts.
