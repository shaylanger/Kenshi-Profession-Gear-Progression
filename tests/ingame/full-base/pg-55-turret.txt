# id: PG-turret-55
# covers: PG 192 (Turrets response, diagnostic first pass)
# fixture: Testing-Save-Full-Base (squad Beaks + Avarek; the base has turrets)
# reset: fresh
# needs: ProfessionGearProgression.dll 7D80DBB3, harness 08AB6BF0 (`protect`, `relation`) + c4fa520 (`pin`); set_test_mode.ps1 -Mode Forced -Rules InGameTest
# m29-4080: operator Beaks; stat asserts wounds=1 (the 0.25 factor was the harness protect bug, fixed in harness 6aa5685: needed).
# design: Beaks mans the nearest turret (its own job). Per point a fresh "Hungry Bandit" (Drifters) is spawned 40 m
#   past the turret and made hostile (`relation` -100: the Drifters faction vs the player), then 45 real seconds at speed 1;
#   the dummy's worst body part % is read. Points: Turrets 10 / 90 without gear, Turrets 10 with +50% Turrets gear.
# verify (diagnostic): the turret fires (dummy worst < 100 or KO/DEAD in at least one point). If it does, worse hp at 90 and at
#   10+gear than at 10 = the turret reads the hooked stat. If the turret never fires (target out of arc, no ammo), send
#   the .out to the PG agent. Squad members are protected; spawned dummies are never next to the squad.
#   m27-4080: the dummies ran off (D2/D3 "no character named" after 45 s, D1 untouched): each is pinned (harness
#   c4fa520 `pin`) on its spawn spot 40 m past the turret until it is read, then unpinned and killed.
@wait-world
@sleep 8
speed 0
protect Avarek on
health Beaks 100
blood Beaks 100%
protect Beaks on
select Avarek
clearjobs Beaks
# m31-5090: m29b FAILed lines power/building "within 60 of the player" (the search origin is the player Avarek,
# ~600 m from the turret): those use radius 1500 too (the Double-Barrel Harpoon Turret is the only match in range).
# m25-4080 probe: the only real turret is the Double-Barrel Harpoon Turret ~570-760 m from the squad (search origin =
# Avarek), unpowered (out_of_power=1); "Training Turret MkIII" is a trainer. Teleport him to it, power it, then the job.
@set TX buildings 1500 harpoon ~ pos=([-\d.]+),[-\d.]+,[-\d.]+
@set TY buildings 1500 harpoon ~ pos=[-\d.]+,([-\d.]+),[-\d.]+
@set TZ buildings 1500 harpoon ~ pos=[-\d.]+,[-\d.]+,([-\d.]+)
@echo turret at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ}
@until 30 teleport Beaks building "Harpoon Turret" dist 4 radius 1500 ~ moved=1
power "Harpoon Turret" supply radius 1500
building "Harpoon Turret" 1500 ~ out_of_power=0
# m25-4080: USE_TURRET is not a permanent job (job ... "job not recorded"; turret never fired): give the real order
order Beaks USE_TURRET building "Harpoon Turret"
speed 1
@sleep 5
speed 0
building "Harpoon Turret" 1500 ~ operators=1
give Beaks "Iron Hat" 1
equip Beaks "Iron Hat"
pg_formulas off ~ formulaScaling=0
pg_clear Beaks ~ cleared affixes
speed 1
@sleep 20
# --- Turrets 10, no gear ---
setstat Beaks turrets 10
stat Beaks turrets ~ wounds=1\.0000
@set D1 spawn "Hungry Bandit" Drifters at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ} dist 40 count 1 ~ spawned 1/1 [^:]+: .+? (#\d+(?:/\d+)?)
pin ${D1} at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ} dist 40 ~ pinned
relation ${D1} -100
@sleep 45
building "Harpoon Turret" 1500 ~ operators=1
@set H1 hp ${D1} ~ (worst=-?[\d.]+%?[^|]*)
pin ${D1} off ~ unpinned
kill ${D1}
# --- Turrets 90, no gear ---
setstat Beaks turrets 90
stat Beaks turrets ~ wounds=1\.0000
@set D2 spawn "Hungry Bandit" Drifters at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ} dist 40 count 1 ~ spawned 1/1 [^:]+: .+? (#\d+(?:/\d+)?)
pin ${D2} at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ} dist 40 ~ pinned
relation ${D2} -100
@sleep 45
building "Harpoon Turret" 1500 ~ operators=1
@set H2 hp ${D2} ~ (worst=-?[\d.]+%?[^|]*)
pin ${D2} off ~ unpinned
kill ${D2}
# --- Turrets 10 with +50% Turrets gear ---
setstat Beaks turrets 10
stat Beaks turrets ~ wounds=1\.0000
pg_force_affix Beaks "Iron Hat" turrets 50 tier 6 ~ affixes=Turrets:50
pg_bonus Beaks turrets ~ equipped_bonus=50% .*match=1
@set D3 spawn "Hungry Bandit" Drifters at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ} dist 40 count 1 ~ spawned 1/1 [^:]+: .+? (#\d+(?:/\d+)?)
pin ${D3} at ${TX} ${TY} ${TZ} dist 40 ~ pinned
relation ${D3} -100
@sleep 45
building "Harpoon Turret" 1500 ~ operators=1
@set H3 hp ${D3} ~ (worst=-?[\d.]+%?[^|]*)
relation ${D3} 0
pin ${D3} off ~ unpinned
kill ${D3}
speed 0
@echo Turrets 10: ${H1}
@echo Turrets 90: ${H2}
@echo Turrets 10 + 50% gear: ${H3}
clearjobs Beaks
